Our promise

Your family's memory is not our product.

  • Private by default. Nothing in your archive is shown to anyone you have not chosen.
  • Never sold, never used for advertising. We earn from plans, books, and the makers, hosts and storytellers you choose to book.
  • Always yours to take away. You can download a complete copy, in an open format, at any time, or delete your account and everything in it.
  • Built to outlast us. Before we charge for anything, we will publish what happens to your archive if The Cultured Human ever closes.
