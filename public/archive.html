Family archive

What you know, kept for those who come next.

Our photos are in the cloud, on hard disks and in albums. But the stories behind them, the tales we were told, the sayings that steadied us and the way a grandparent laughed live only in our memories. Keep them here, in your own words and voice, for the generations after you.

Family accounts are coming. Until then, your archive is saved in this browser on this device. Use Save a copy to keep a backup or to send it to a relative. About family accounts.

The weekly question

One question a week. A year of their voice.

Send an elder one question each week on WhatsApp. They reply with a voice note, in whichever language feels right, whenever they like. You keep the reply here. Fifty-two weeks later, you have a year of their stories, sayings and wisdom, enough for a book.

  1. Tell us who you're asking.
  2. Send this week's question with one tap.
  3. Save their voice note when it arrives.

What your family has kept

What to keep

Everything in between the photographs.

  1. Stories and tales

    The tale you asked for every night. How they met. Why the family moved.

  2. Moments together

    A photograph, and what was happening just outside the frame.

  3. Wisdom and sayings

    The proverb your grandmother repeated, and what she meant by it.

  4. For hard days

    What helped them stay calm, grounded and confident when life was uncertain.

  5. Letters to the future

    Words for someone not yet grown, or not yet born: open when you leave home, open when you feel lost.

About storytellers
  • Storytellers on the platform sit with your elder, in person or online, in the language they are most at ease in.
  • Everything they record goes into your family's archive, and your family owns it.
  • Printed books can be made from any archive, whether you recorded it or they did.
