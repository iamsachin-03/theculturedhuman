Terms

How it works between us.

By using The Cultured Human you agree to these terms. We have kept them short and plain. Please also read our privacy page and community guidelines.

Last updated 18 November 2026.

Your account

You sign in with your email address. Keep access to that email safe, because anyone who can read it can sign in as you. You must be an adult to have an account.

What you add stays yours

Your family archive, and everything in it, belongs to you. We store it so you, and the people you choose, can see it. We don't use it for anything else.

When you share something with The Commons, you still own it. You give us permission to show it on the site, with the credit you chose. We will only share it on our social media if we ask you first and you say yes. You can change or delete it at any time, and we stop showing it on the site.

What you may share

Share only what is yours to share: your own words, your family's memories, or something you have permission to share. Don't share anything private about a person who hasn't agreed. Everything you share with The Commons must follow the community guidelines. We read everything before it is published, and we may decline or remove anything that doesn't follow them, with a reason.

Makers, hosts and storytellers

  • Listings are read before they go up. Describe your work honestly, and only offer what you can do yourself.
  • You set your own price for each request. When the family accepts your quote, they pay the price plus a platform fee of 10%, shown to them before they accept. You receive your full quoted price.
  • You are responsible for the work you agree to do, and for any taxes on what you earn.

Families who book

  • Nothing is charged until you accept a quote. The quote shows the maker's, host's or storyteller's price and our fee separately.
  • Makers, hosts and storytellers are independent people, not our employees. We read their listings, but we can't guarantee their work. If something goes wrong, tell us and we will help.

What we promise, and what we can't

We work hard to keep the site running and your archive safe. But no website can promise to be available every moment. We are not responsible for losses caused by things outside our control. Keep your own copies of anything irreplaceable.

Ending things

You can delete your account at any time from Settings. We may close an account that repeatedly breaks these terms or the community guidelines, after telling you why.

If The Cultured Human itself ever closes, our promise says what happens to your archive: at least 90 days' notice, a full copy, refunds, nothing sold, and everything deleted afterwards.

Changes

If we change these terms, we will update this page and the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will email you.
