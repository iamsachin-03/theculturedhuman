Start a commission

We are inviting our first artists now. Send us your idea and we will reply with who could make it, how long it might take and what it would cost. Nothing is charged until you have agreed a sketch and a price.

How it works

From an idea to a finished piece.

  1. Share your idea

    The occasion, the size or space, a rough budget and when you need it.

  2. Meet the artist

    We introduce a named artist and show you examples of their work.

  3. A clear price

    The artist sets their price. Our fee is added and shown separately.

  4. Approve a sketch

    You see the composition before the work begins.

  5. Delivered with its story

    Your piece arrives with a card about the artist and their tradition.

Our promise to artists and to you

Fair to the maker. Honest with the buyer.

How we share money
  • Every artist is named and credited, on the piece's card and anywhere we share it.
  • The artist sets the price. Our fee is never hidden inside it.
  • No copies. We do not reproduce a living artist's work or sell imitations of a tradition.
  • Honest timelines. Handmade work takes time, and we tell you how long before you commit.
