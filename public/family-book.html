Free, at home

Print it yourself, as often as you like.

Open your archive as a book, choose the chapters, and print it or save it as a PDF from your browser. Add a story next month and print it again. Nothing to pay and nothing to install.

Recordings appear as what was said in them, with an English translation underneath for the grandchildren who don't speak the language. Type a transcript yourself, or switch on automatic transcripts.

A bound copy

One for every home in the family.

  1. Tell us about it

    Roughly how many entries and photographs, how many copies, and the date you need them by: a birthday, an anniversary, a wedding.

  2. You send the PDF

    Saved from your archive. We check the photographs will print well and tell you if any are too small.

  3. A proof first

    You see every page before anything is printed, and the price before you agree.

  4. Printed and bound

    Hardback, on paper meant to last, posted to you or straight to each home.

Our promise

Only what you choose goes into the book.

  • You pick the chapters. Letters left for later stay out unless you tick them, and you can leave out anything kept as Only me.
  • We see only the PDF you send, never your archive, and delete it once the books are delivered.
  • Their words are never rewritten. We may fix a typing slip if you ask, nothing more.
