Community guidelines

Be curious, be generous.

The Commons only works if everyone feels safe bringing something personal. A person reads everything before it appears. These are the things they look for.

Share what is yours to share

  • Your own words, your family's memories and recipes, words your grandparents used.
  • Ask before writing about a living person, and leave out names, addresses and other details that could identify someone who hasn't agreed.
  • Don't copy someone else's writing, photographs or recordings. Quote a line or two and say where it's from.

Say where you heard it

"My grandmother said" is a source. So is a book, a song or a village elder. It helps others trust what you share, and helps them find their own version.

Nothing hostile

We don't publish anything hostile to any caste, community, faith, gender or region, or anything that mocks how another family lives. Places are remembered differently by different families, and both can be true. Disagree kindly.

No selling here

The Commons isn't for advertising. Makers, hosts and storytellers can join and offer their work properly instead.

Words, done well

For a word or saying, write it the way it sounds in English letters, add it in its own script if you can, say how it's pronounced, and tell us what it means and when your family uses it.

When something isn't published

If we don't publish something, you will see why in what you have shared, and we will email you. You can change it and send it again.

Reporting

If you see something that breaks these guidelines, choose Report under it. A moderator reads every report. The person who shared it is not told who reported it. If it breaks the guidelines, we take it down and tell them why.

Spotted a mistake rather than a problem? Send it as "A correction" from the place's page, and we will fix it with credit.

Fair limits

To keep The Commons from being flooded, one person can share up to ten things a day. We may close accounts that repeatedly break these guidelines.

See also our terms and privacy page.
