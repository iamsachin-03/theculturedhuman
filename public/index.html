A thought to carry

“Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam” — the world is one family.

A phrase from the Maha Upanishad that invites us to look beyond narrow divisions and recognise our shared humanity. We explore Indian ideas in their original context, and ask what they can mean in the world we live in today.
