Your roots · Family stories

Keep the stories your family carries.

The recipe nobody wrote down. The festival as it was celebrated in the village. The journey that brought the family where it is. We record these memories with care and turn them into a keepsake for everyone who comes next.

Ask about family stories

What your family receives

A book to hold, and a voice to hear.

  • The recordings, so the voice and the way of telling are kept as well as the words.
  • A printed booklet of the stories, recipes and rituals, with your family's photographs.
  • A digital copy to share with relatives anywhere in the world.

How it works

Gentle, unhurried and in their own words.

  1. A first conversation

    With you, to understand the person, the family and what matters most to keep.

  2. Recorded conversations

    At home or online, at their pace, in the language they are most at ease in wherever we can.

  3. Writing and design

    We transcribe, edit and design the booklet. The family reviews everything before it is printed.

  4. The keepsake

    The booklet, the recordings and a digital copy, delivered to you.

Our promise

Your family's stories stay your family's.

  • You own everything: the recordings, the text and the photographs.
  • Nothing is published without your written permission. Most families keep their book private.
  • Their words, not ours. We edit for clarity, never to change what someone meant.
  • A thoughtful gift for a milestone birthday or anniversary, or for relatives living far from home.
  • Prefer to do it yourself? The Roots Kit gives you the questions and templates.
