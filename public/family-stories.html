Family archive · Storytellers

When you can't ask the questions yourself.

You live far away. The language isn't quite yours any more. Or it simply feels too close. Storytellers on The Cultured Human sit with your elder, record their stories, tales and sayings with care, and add everything to your family's archive.

Request a storyteller

What your family receives

A voice to hear, and a book to hold.

  • The recordings, so the voice and the way of telling are kept as well as the words.
  • Everything added to your family archive, sorted into stories, wisdom and moments together.
  • A printed book of the stories, recipes and sayings with your photographs, if you would like one.

How it works

Gentle, unhurried and in their own words.

  1. Tell us about them

    Who they are, where they live and the languages they speak.

  2. Meet your storyteller

    We introduce a storyteller on the platform who speaks their language and lives nearby or works online. Their price is shown before you agree.

  3. The conversations

    At home or online, at your elder's pace, starting from the questions your family chooses.

  4. Kept, for good

    Recordings and transcripts go into your archive. Your family reviews everything before any book is printed.

Our promise

Your family's stories stay your family's.

  • You own everything: the recordings, the text and the photographs.
  • Consent comes first. Storytellers ask your elder's permission before recording, and stop when asked.
  • Their words, not ours. Transcripts are edited for clarity, never to change what someone meant.
  • Storytellers set their own price. The platform fee is shown separately.
