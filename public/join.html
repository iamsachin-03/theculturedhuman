How it works

From your listing to your first family.

  1. List yourself

    Sign in with your email and describe what you offer, where and in which languages. It takes about ten minutes and costs nothing.

  2. We read it

    A person reads every listing before it is public, so families can trust who they meet.

  3. Families find you

    Your listing appears on the page of your place, and on Keepsakes, Journeys or Storytellers. Families send you a request.

  4. You quote

    Reply with your price and a note. You see exactly what the family will pay before you send it.

  5. They accept

    Once a family accepts, you each see the other's email to plan the details. The family pays through The Cultured Human.

Your price, in full

The fee is on top, never taken from you.

Families see your price and our 10% fee as separate lines on every quote. Listing is free, and there is no subscription.

What we look for

Real work, honestly described.

  • Your own work. No resold goods, and no copies of a living tradition or another artist's designs.
  • A place you know. Hosts live in or come from the place they list; makers and storytellers name the tradition and the place it belongs to.
  • Honest timings. Say how long things take, and tell families early if something changes.
  • Respect for families. Their stories, photographs and contact details are theirs. Use them only for the work they asked for.

Questions

Before you list.

Does it cost anything to list?

No. Listing is free and there is no subscription. The platform earns a 10% fee, paid by the family on top of your price, only when a booking goes ahead.

Who sets the price?

You do, for every request. You can add a price guide to your listing, such as "From ₹2,500 a session", so families know what to expect before they ask.

How do families contact me?

They send a request through the platform. Your email is shared only with a family who has accepted your quote, and theirs only with you.

How am I paid?

The family pays the total through The Cultured Human before the work begins. You receive your full quoted price once the work is delivered or the journey has taken place.

Can I change my listing?

Yes, at any time from your account. Changes are read before they are public, like new listings.

My place isn't on the site yet.

Add your place with one thing families from there carry. Places with a few contributions get their own page, and then you can list yourself there.
