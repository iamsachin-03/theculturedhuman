Join the platform

Families are looking for the people who know.

The painter who still works in the old style. The guide who can find the house a grandfather left. The storyteller who can sit with an elder in their own language. The Cultured Human connects families with them.

Makers Hosts Storytellers

How it works for you

Your work, your name, your price.

How money is shared
  • You set your price. The platform fee is added on top and shown separately to the family.
  • Your name is on everything: your listing, your place's page and every piece or journey.
  • Families find you through their places. Your listing appears on the page of every place you work in.
  • Every listing is reviewed before it appears, so families can trust who they meet.

For makers

Make things that carry someone's roots.

Painters, weavers, potters, cooks and writers whose work carries a tradition forward. Families commission paintings of their home towns, their family name in a folk style and gifts with a story.

Your craft, where you work, how long you have practised, and a link to photographs of your work if you have one.

We use your details only to reply to you.

For hosts

Welcome families coming home.

People who know a town or village well and can help a family find the street, the temple, the school or the relatives they have only heard about, or share a day of a tradition they practise.

Where you live, the languages you speak, and what a family visiting with you would experience.

We use your details only to reply to you.

For storytellers

Help a family keep a voice.

Oral historians, journalists, producers and community volunteers who can record an elder with patience and care, in the language they are most at ease in, and help the family turn it into a book.

The languages you can record in, where you are based, and any recordings or writing we could listen to or read.

We use your details only to reply to you.

For organisations

Bring a whole group.

Schools, diaspora associations and workplaces can run roots programmes with the platform's tools and people.
