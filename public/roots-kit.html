Your roots · The Roots Kit

Ask, record, keep.

Everything you need to capture your elders' stories yourself, at the kitchen table or over a video call from the other side of the world.

Join the waitlist

The Roots Kit is being made. Join the waitlist and we will write when it is ready, with an early price for the first families.

What's inside

A gentle structure for a big conversation.

  1. Question cards

    Prompts grouped by theme: home, food, festivals, journeys, names and the things worth passing on.

  2. A recording guide

    How to record clearly on a phone, ask permission and keep the files safe.

  3. A family-book template

    Pages to fill with stories, recipes, photographs and your own roots portrait.

  4. A family tree of places

    A printable version of Trace your roots, for the wall or the album.

Who it's for

For anyone who wishes they had asked sooner.

  • Families living apart, in another city or another country, who talk on video calls.
  • Grandchildren who want a reason to sit with their grandparents and listen.
  • Parents who want their children to know where the family comes from.
  • Anyone preparing a gift for a milestone birthday or anniversary.
