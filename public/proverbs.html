Bhagavad Gita · 2.47
“You have a right to action, but not to the fruits of action.”

A concise translation of a verse often discussed in the context of karma yoga. Translations vary.

The idea

Do the work. Loosen your grip.

Krishna's teaching to Arjuna is not an invitation to indifference. It asks us to act with care and responsibility without making our worth depend entirely on outcomes. The verse sits within a larger conversation about duty, discernment and disciplined action.

Bring it into life

Effort with steadiness.

Prepare well for the work in front of you. Give it your attention. Learn from what happens, but do not let every result define you. This is one possible contemporary reflection — not a substitute for studying the text in context.
