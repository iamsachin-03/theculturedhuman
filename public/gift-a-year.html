How it works

A card now, a question every week.

Tell us who it is for, their WhatsApp number, the day of the week and whether to ask in English or Hindi. You get a card to print and give by hand, and a message that tells them what is coming, ready to send.

Then, every week on their day, a new question: where they grew up, the people they loved, what they would tell a grandchild. Their voice notes are kept in your family archive, private until you choose who sees them.

  1. Make the card

    Their name, the occasion and a first question as a taste. Print it, or send the message on WhatsApp.

  2. A question a week

    Sent from your phone in one tap, or for you from our number once they have agreed.

  3. Their voice, kept

    Each answer is kept in your archive, with a transcript and an English translation if you want one.

  4. A book at the end

    After a year, their answers make a family book to print at home or have bound.

Our promise

Their stories stay the family's.

About the family book
  • Free to give. The questions, the card and keeping the answers cost nothing.
  • Nothing goes to them without their say. Our number only messages them after you tell us they have agreed.
  • Never used to train AI, and their voice is never copied. The recordings are theirs and yours.
