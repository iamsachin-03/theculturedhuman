My archive

What we offer

Everyone comes from somewhere. Here is how we help you keep it.

Finding where your family comes from, keeping the voices and stories that go with it, and passing them on. Start with whichever is nearest to you. Most of it is free, and nothing is shared unless you choose.

1 Discover 2 Keep 3 Give and go back 4 Together

Not sure where to begin?

  • “I want to keep my grandmother’s voice.”Start the weekly question in your family archive.
  • “I only know the name of our village.”Trace your roots, free and without an account.
  • “I want to give something that means something.”A keepsake or a commission from a named maker.
  • “I want to go back.”A journey with a host who lives there.

3 Give and go back

Hold it, give it, walk it.

Objects made by named makers, and journeys with hosts who live in the places families come from.

Keepsakes

Objects that carry your roots: a painting of your family's place, your roots portrait made into art, and small drops from named makers.

Each maker's own price, plus a 10% fee shown separately.Find out more

Commission a piece

A painting of your family's home town or your family name in a folk-art style, made by a named artist who sets the price.

The artist's own price, plus a 10% fee shown separately.Find out more

Gifting

Festive, wedding and company gifts from named makers, made to pre-order, each with a card that tells its story.

Quoted per order, with the makers' prices shown.Find out more

Journeys

Go back to your family's village or town with a host who knows it, or spend a few days with a tradition and the people who keep it.
