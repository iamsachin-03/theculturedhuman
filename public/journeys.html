Before you go

The more you know, the more a host can find.

Most journeys begin with a half-remembered name. These free steps turn it into somewhere a host can take you.

  1. Trace

    Draw the places your family has lived, generation by generation, in Trace your roots. It shows where the trail goes quiet.

  2. Ask

    Ask an elder for the village, the street, the temple, the family name. Twelve questions help, or send them one a week.

  3. Read the place

    See what other families from there carry on its place page, and add what you learn.

  4. Go back

    Tell us what you found below, and we'll introduce you to a host there.

How journeys work

Be a guest, not just a visitor.

How we share money
  • Your family, or a small group, never a crowd.
  • Hosts are named, reviewed and set their own rates, shown before you book.
  • Group journeys with travel and stays are run with an established, insured travel partner, named before you book.
  • Respect comes first: ask before photographing people. Nothing is staged as a performance for visitors.
