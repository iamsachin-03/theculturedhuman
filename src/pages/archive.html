Family archive

What you know, kept for those who come next.

Our photos are in the cloud, on hard disks and in albums. But the stories behind them, the tales we were told, the sayings that steadied us and the way a grandparent laughed live only in our memories. Keep them in your own private archive, in your family's words and voices.

What to keep

Everything in between the photographs.

  1. Stories and tales

    The tale you asked for every night. How they met. Why the family moved. Written, or in their own voice.

  2. Moments together

    Photographs and videos, and what was happening just outside the frame.

  3. Wisdom and sayings

    The proverb your grandmother repeated, and what she meant by it.

  4. For hard days

    What helped them stay calm, grounded and confident when life was uncertain.

  5. Letters to the future

    Words for someone not yet grown, or not yet born: open when you leave home, open when you feel lost.

Old certificates, letters, land records and family documents can be kept too, with the story of what they mean.

The weekly question

One question a week. A year of their voice.

Send an elder one question each week on WhatsApp. They reply with a voice note, in whichever language feels right, whenever they like. You keep the reply in your archive. Fifty-two weeks later, you have a year of their stories, sayings and wisdom, enough for a book.

Who sees what

You choose, for every single entry.

Only me
Stays private forever, even after your archive is passed on.
My family
Everyone you have added as family, as soon as you keep it. Family who can add may keep their own stories in your archive too.
Specific people
Only the people you choose.
Specific people, on a date
A letter for a granddaughter's eighteenth birthday opens on that day, and not before.
When my archive is passed on
Nobody sees it until then. Then your beloved ones, or the people you chose.

Passing it on

Nothing is passed on while you are still here.

  • Choose a legacy contact, someone you trust to tell us when you have died.
  • We wait before anything is passed on, 30 days by default. If you sign in during that time, it is cancelled.
  • If you go quiet for a long time, we send you reminders first, and only then ask your legacy contact.
  • "Only me" stays private, and letters with a date still wait for their day.

About storytellers
  • Storytellers on the platform sit with your elder, in person or online, in the language they are most at ease in.
  • Everything they record goes into your family's archive, and your family owns it.
  • Printed books can be made from any archive, whether you recorded it or they did.
