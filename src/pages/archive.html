Family archive

What you know, kept for those who come next.

Our photos are in the cloud, on hard disks and in albums. But the stories behind them, the tales we were told, the sayings that steadied us and the way a grandparent laughed live only in our memories. Keep them here, in your own words and voice, for the generations after you.

What to keep

Everything in between the photographs.

  1. Stories and tales

    The tale you asked for every night. How they met. Why the family moved.

  2. Moments together

    A photograph, and what was happening just outside the frame.

  3. Wisdom and sayings

    The proverb your grandmother repeated, and what she meant by it.

  4. For hard days

    What helped them stay calm, grounded and confident when life was uncertain.

  5. Letters to the future

    Words for someone not yet grown, or not yet born: open when you leave home, open when you feel lost.
