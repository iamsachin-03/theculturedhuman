Trace your roots

The deeper you go, the older the story.

Add the places your family has called home, from you down to the oldest generation you know. Your portrait grows as you type. Draw it as roots, a tree, a river, tree rings or a sky of stars, then download and share it.

Keep in my archive

Everything you type stays in this browser. Nothing is sent to us unless you choose to keep the portrait in your family archive, where only you can see it and you can open it again on any device.

Ask a relative to fill in the gaps

Send your portrait to a parent, an aunt or a grandparent. They can add what they remember on their phone and send it back to you the same way.

Send on WhatsApp

The link carries the portrait itself, so it never passes through us. Anyone you send it to can see these places, so send it only to family.
