How it works

Ask. Keep. Pass it on.

  1. {{img ask-a-question sizes="(max-width: 860px) 100vw, 360px"}}

    Ask

    One question a week to a parent or grandparent, sent on WhatsApp. They reply with a voice note whenever they like. In a year, their life in their own words.

  2. {{img keepsake-box sizes="(max-width: 860px) 100vw, 360px"}}

    Keep

    Stories and tales, voice notes and videos, photographs and what happened outside the frame, sayings, things for hard days, and old documents. All in one private place.

  3. {{img passing-it-on sizes="(max-width: 860px) 100vw, 360px"}}

    Pass it on

    Share with family now. Leave letters for a grandchild's eighteenth birthday. Choose what your beloved ones receive when you are gone, and who confirms when it is time.

Our promise

Built to be trusted with a family's memory.

  • Private by default. Nobody sees an entry unless you share it with them. "Only me" stays private forever.
  • Never sold. No advertising, and your family's stories are never sold.
  • Always yours. Download everything at any time, or delete your account and everything in it.
  • Built to be passed on. Nothing is passed on while you are still visiting, and only to the people you chose.
