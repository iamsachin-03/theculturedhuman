Where are your roots?

A place to share one thing your family carries, and to discover what everyone else's carries too.

This week's prompt

Which word from your family has no translation?

Every week there is one prompt about roots: a dish, a ritual, a journey, a name. Answers are added to the page of the place they come from, credited the way each person chooses.

What to bring

A word, a dish, a place.

Prompts to come include: the dish that means home, the object your family kept when it moved, the festival as your grandparents celebrated it, and the story behind your family name. Be curious, be generous, and leave room to learn.

How it will open

Slowly, and safely.

Everything shared is read before it appears, and anything hostile to a caste, community or faith is removed. Place pages are open now; profiles and replies will follow with family accounts.
