How it works

Share one thing. It finds its place.

  1. Answer

    Sign in with your email, no password, and share a word, a dish, a ritual, a song or a memory. Choose the place it comes from and how you want to be credited.

  2. We read it

    A person reads everything before it appears, and we email you when it does. You can see, change or delete what you've shared in your account.

  3. It joins a place

    Once published, it appears on the page of its place, here in The Commons, and on its own page, with a card you can share with your family.

House rules

Be curious, be generous.

The Commons only works if everyone feels safe bringing something personal. These are the few things we ask. The full community guidelines say more.

  • Share what is yours to share. Ask before writing about a living person, and leave out details that could identify someone who hasn't agreed.
  • Say where you heard it. "My grandmother said" is a source; so is a book or a song.
  • Nothing hostile to any caste, community, faith, gender or region is published.
  • Disagree kindly. Places are remembered differently by different families, and both can be true.
  • Spot a mistake? Send it as "A correction" from the place's page, and we'll fix it with credit. See something that breaks these rules? Choose Report under it, and a moderator will read it.
