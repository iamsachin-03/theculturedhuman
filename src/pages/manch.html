Community · The Commons

Where are your roots?

A place to share one thing your family carries, and to discover what everyone else's carries too.

This week's prompt

Which word from your family has no translation?

Every week we share one prompt about roots: a dish, a ritual, a journey, a name. Answers become a living collection of where we all come from, shared only with permission.

What to bring

A word, a dish, a place.

Prompts to come include: the dish that means home, the object your family kept when it moved, the festival as your grandparents celebrated it, and the story behind your family name. Be curious, be generous, and leave room to learn.

How it will open

Slowly, and safely.

Community features will open in stages. We will prioritise thoughtful participation, moderation and member safety over vanity metrics. There is no sign-up yet.
