Gifts that arrive with a story.

For Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, weddings and company gifting: objects from named makers, chosen with care, each with a card about the person who made it and the craft behind it.

Made to pre-order

Nothing made for a warehouse.

Each collection opens for pre-orders with a clear deadline. Makers are paid for work that is already wanted, and nothing is made only to sit unsold.

Where a product carries a Geographical Indication, the legal recognition that ties a name to its place and its producers, we buy it from the people entitled to make it.

Read about GI-tagged foods

What is in a box

Small, thoughtful and traceable.

  1. Two to four objects

    From named makers, chosen to belong together, never filler.

  2. A story card

    Who made each piece, where, and what the craft means.

  3. Careful packing

  4. Your own note

    A handwritten message from you, or a card with your company's message.
