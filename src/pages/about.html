Our story

Everyone comes from somewhere.

The Cultured Human helps people reconnect with their roots: the languages, recipes, rituals, crafts and places their families carry. We begin in India, where our own roots and our makers are, and we welcome everyone, wherever their family began.

Why we exist

Because the stories fade when nobody asks.

Culture is not only in museums. It is in a grandmother's kitchen, a song sung at weddings, a word that has no translation, a village someone left fifty years ago. When families move, these things are easy to lose. Our work is to help people find them, keep them and see them in a new light.

Our mission

Help people find their way back.

Give people simple ways to trace and keep their own family's story; publish researched stories about the traditions families carry; and connect people with the makers, places and experiences that keep those traditions alive.

Our vision

Rooted, and open to the world.

A world where knowing where you come from makes you more curious about where everyone else comes from, not less. Every family's story is part of the same larger one.

Our editorial promise

Substance before aesthetics.

Context and credible sources before quotations. Curiosity without preaching. Respect without flattening differences. Quality over frequency. We want to explain, not merely decorate; invite reflection, not dictate beliefs.

How we work

Credit the makers. Cite the sources.

Every article lists its sources. Every picture is an original illustration, drawn for the story it sits beside — you can see them all on our illustrations page. When we get something wrong, we correct it.

Honest commerce

How we share money.

Tracing, keeping and reading are free. The platform earns from family plans and printed books, and a fee on what families book with makers, hosts and storytellers. Here is exactly how that money is shared.

  • Commissions: the artist sets the price. Our fee is added on top and shown separately on every quote.
  • Sessions for organisations: the practitioner's fee is agreed in writing before booking and shown separately from ours.
  • Gifts and drops: every maker is named and paid the price they ask. Pre-orders mean nobody makes work that goes unsold.
  • Journeys: local hosts are named and paid at rates agreed before the journey, and our travel partner is named before you book.
  • Family archives: your family owns everything in them. Archives are private by default, never sold and never used for advertising.
  • Makers, hosts and storytellers list for free and set their own prices. The platform fee of {{fee-percent}}% is added on top for the family and shown separately, so they receive their full price.
  • Sponsorship: any sponsored work is clearly labelled and is never presented as one of our articles.
