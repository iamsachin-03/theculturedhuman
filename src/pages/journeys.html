Go back to where it began.

Visit your family's village or town with someone to guide the way, or spend a few days with a tradition and the people who keep it. Small groups, local hosts who are paid fairly, and time to listen rather than rush.

Your own roots journey

Find the house, the temple, the street.

Tell us what you know: a village name, a family name, an old photograph. We help you plan a visit, find local guides and, where it is possible, the people and places connected to your family. Nothing is promised that can't be found, and we tell you honestly what is likely.

How our journeys work

Be a guest, not just a visitor.

How we share money
  • Small groups of around eight to twelve people.
  • Local hosts are named and paid at rates agreed before the journey.
  • Travel, stays and safety are handled by an established, insured travel partner, named before you book.
  • Respect comes first: we ask before photographing people and never stage a performance for visitors.
